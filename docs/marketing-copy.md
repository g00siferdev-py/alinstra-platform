1. **Tell us about your business.** Hours, services, who to transfer to, what to say when you're closed. Takes about twenty minutes.
2. **Forward your missed calls.** Keep your number. Your carrier sends unanswered calls to Ava.
3. **Get every message instantly.** Caller, callback number, what they need, by email or text the moment the call ends.

### Ava calls them back, too.
Flea and tick refills. Annual vaccines. Grooming. Furnace tune-ups. Every business with a repeat schedule loses revenue to customers who simply forgot. Give Ava your recall list and she calls when it's due, offers the next appointment, and books it. One reminder call that lands is worth the whole month.
*Available as the Follow-up add-on. Customers must have agreed to be contacted by phone.*

### What Ava does
- Answers in two rings, 24 hours a day
- Takes messages and schedules appointments: as a request your office confirms, or booked straight into your calendar when it's connected
- Makes follow-up calls from your recall list, so the customer books the next visit before they forget
- Transfers urgent calls to a real person during business hours
- Knows your hours, services, and policies, and says "I don't know" instead of guessing
- Never shares a staff member's cell number, and tells callers the truth when asked if she's a person
